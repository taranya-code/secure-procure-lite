What is SecureProcure?

SecureProcure is an enterprise procurement management system designed to bring transparency, control, and auditability to an organisation's purchasing process. It digitises the entire journey — from an employee raising a purchase request to a vendor being paid — replacing ad-hoc emails and spreadsheets with a structured, role-based workflow.

The system enforces a strict chain of approvals, maintains a tamper-evident audit log of every action, and uses AI-assisted analysis to help procurement officers select the best vendor quote. All monetary amounts are tracked in INR (₹), suitable for Indian organisations.

Key benefits: Prevent unauthorised purchases · Ensure multi-level approvals · Maintain a full audit trail for compliance · Streamline vendor comparison with AI scoring · Block self-approval fraud automatically.

User Roles
🔴
Admin
Full system access. Manages user accounts (create, edit, activate/deactivate, reset passwords). Can do everything a Procurement Officer and Approver can do. Accesses the Admin Panel for database management and this Docs page. Responsible for system health and user governance.
🔵
Procurement Officer
Creates and manages Purchase Requests (PRs). Submits PRs for approval. Once a PR is approved, issues RFQs (Request for Quotation) to vendors. Reviews vendor quotes, runs AI analysis, selects the winning quote, and issues the Purchase Order (PO). Cannot approve their own requests.
🟢
Approver
Reviews submitted Purchase Requests and either approves or rejects them with a comment. Cannot approve requests they created themselves (self-approval is blocked and logged as a fraud alert). Sees a count of pending approvals in the sidebar badge.
⚫
Auditor
Read-only access for compliance and governance. Views all audit logs, purchase requests, RFQs, and vendor data. Cannot create, edit, or approve anything. Uses the Audit Logs page to review every action taken in the system, including fraud alert entries.
🟡
Vendor
External supplier who receives RFQs and submits quotations. Logs in to see their assigned RFQs and submit quotes with pricing, delivery terms, and validity. Can only see their own quotes — not other vendors' bids.

Approval Process — Who Approves What

Purchase Requests (PRs) require approval from a user with the Approver or Admin role. The officer who created the PR cannot approve it — this is a hard system rule enforced at every attempt, and any bypass attempt is permanently logged as a fraud alert.

RFQs do not require a separate approval step. Once the PR is approved, the Procurement Officer has authority to issue an RFQ directly. The approval of the PR implicitly authorises the procurement up to the estimated budget.

Quote selection / PO issuance is the responsibility of the Procurement Officer (or Admin). No additional approval is required to select a vendor once the RFQ process is complete — the selection is logged in the audit trail.

Fraud Prevention: The system automatically blocks any attempt by a user to approve a request they created. The attempt is recorded in the audit log as APPROVAL_SELF_APPROVAL_BLOCKED and is visible to Auditors and Admins.
Quick Start Guide — New to the App?
If you are a Procurement Officer:
  1. Log in → go to Purchase Requests → click + New Request
  2. Fill in title, category, budget, department, and add at least one line item → Save as Draft
  3. Open your draft → click Submit for Approval
  4. Wait for an Approver to review. You'll get a notification when a decision is made.
  5. Once approved, open the request → click Issue RFQ → select vendors → set deadline → submit
  6. Vendors will log in and submit quotes. Monitor the RFQ page for incoming quotes.
  7. When quotes are in, click Analyse Quotes for AI scoring → review scores → click Select on the best quote → PO is issued
If you are an Approver:
  1. Log in → go to Approvals (the sidebar badge shows how many are pending)
  2. Click on a pending request to review the details, budget justification, and line items
  3. Click Approve to proceed, or Reject with a reason
  4. Your decision is logged in the audit trail immediately
If you are a Vendor:
  1. Log in → go to My Quotes
  2. You'll see all RFQs you've been invited to respond to
  3. Click on an RFQ → fill in your quote amount, delivery days, validity period, and any notes
  4. Submit your quote before the deadline
If you are an Auditor:
  1. Log in → go to Audit Logs to review all system actions
  2. Use filters to narrow by action type, entity, or date range
  3. View Purchase Requests, RFQ & Quotations, and Vendors in read-only mode
  4. Check Reports for spend analytics and compliance metrics
